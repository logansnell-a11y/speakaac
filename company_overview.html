Company Overview · May 2026

Speak AAC is a browser-based augmentative and alternative communication platform designed for children and adults with autism, Down syndrome, cerebral palsy, stroke, ALS, and other communication differences. The core app is free for families, forever. Clinic and institution tiers provide provider dashboards, multi-patient management, and HIPAA-compliant data handling. Speak runs on any device — no app store, no proprietary hardware, no specialist required to set up.

Federal Grant Eligibility — SBIR / NIDILRR

Speak AAC is registered in SAM.gov and eligible for SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research) funding through NIDILRR — the National Institute on Disability, Independent Living, and Rehabilitation Research. Phase I: $150,000 · Phase II: up to $1,000,000. The September 5, 2026 deadline is the target submission. SBIR applications are significantly strengthened by letters of support from licensed clinical partners and by pilot usage data from real patients. Early clinic partners are positioned as co-development contributors in the application narrative.
