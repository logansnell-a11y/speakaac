**3.2 Work Product Defined.** "Work Product" means any and all inventions, discoveries, improvements, processes, methods, algorithms, software (source code and object code), databases, designs, prototypes, drawings, documentation, works of authorship, and other intellectual property that Contractor creates, conceives, develops, or reduces to practice — alone or jointly with others — during the term of this Agreement that:

(b) result from or relate to any Services performed for the Company; or  
(c) are made using any of the Company's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information.

**3.3 Assignment — "Hereby Assigns."** Contractor **hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys** to the Company, and its successors and assigns, all right, title, and interest in and to all Work Product throughout the world and in perpetuity, including:

- all patent rights (including the right to file, prosecute, maintain, and enforce patents and provisional patent applications);
- all copyright rights (including all rights of reproduction, distribution, adaptation, and public display);
- all trade secret rights;
- all trademark and trade dress rights; and
- all other intellectual property rights.

This assignment is effective as of the moment of conception or creation of the Work Product, without any further act required by either Party.

**3.4 Work Made for Hire.** To the extent any Work Product constitutes a "work made for hire" within the meaning of the U.S. Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 101), such Work Product is and shall be considered a work made for hire owned exclusively by the Company. To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as a work made for hire, Contractor's assignment under Section 3.3 shall apply.

**3.5 Cooperation.** Contractor shall, during and after the term of this Agreement, at the Company's reasonable request and expense, execute all documents, provide all information, and take all actions that the Company may deem necessary or useful to:

(a) apply for, obtain, and maintain patent, copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property protection for the Work Product in any jurisdiction;  
(b) perfect, confirm, or record the Company's ownership of the Work Product; and  
(c) enforce the Company's intellectual property rights against infringement.

The Company may, as Contractor's attorney-in-fact (and Contractor hereby grants such limited power of attorney, which is coupled with an interest), execute any documents required to effectuate this Section 3.5 if Contractor is unavailable or refuses to do so.

**3.6 Moral Rights Waiver.** To the extent permitted by applicable law, Contractor waives and agrees not to assert any moral rights, droit moral, or similar rights in the Work Product.

**3.7 No License to Contractor.** Contractor acknowledges that the assignment under this Section 3 is complete and unconditional, and Contractor retains no license, right, or interest in any Work Product following such assignment.
