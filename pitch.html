Safety
The feature no other
AAC tool has built.
Children who can't speak are at higher risk of abuse. Speak gives them a way to ask for help silently.

Private Safety Channel

  • A discreet button the child can tap without the caretaker noticing
  • Sends a silent email alert to a pre-configured trusted adult
  • Works offline — queued for delivery when connection resumes
  • Cannot be disabled without the Provider PIN
  • No visual confirmation shown — the child's safety is the priority

Why This Matters

  • Children with communication disabilities are 3–4× more likely to experience abuse
  • Traditional AAC tools have no safety feature at all
  • Parents and SLPs consistently cite this as the most important differentiator
  • Designed in consultation with safeguarding best practices
