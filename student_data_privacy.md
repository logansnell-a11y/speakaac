# Speak — Student Data Privacy (FERPA / COPPA) — District-Facing Summary

> ⚠️ **DRAFT TEMPLATE — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Have an attorney review before publishing or relying on it with a district. This is meant to (a) become a "Trust & Privacy" page at speakaac.org/privacy and (b) be handed to schools/SLPs who ask "is student data safe?"

## Our commitment
Speak is built to help nonverbal students communicate. Protecting their data is part of that mission. We collect the **minimum** needed to run the service, we **never sell it**, and we **never use it for advertising.**

## What we collect
- **Account info:** a name or username, the caregiver/SLP's email, and subscription tier.
- **Usage data:** which symbols/words are selected, messages spoken, AI sentences generated, session activity and timestamps, and an optional communicator identifier the provider sets.
- We do **not** collect precise location, government IDs, or store biometric identifiers on our servers.

## How we use it
Only to provide and improve the Speak service for the student and their team — to make the app work, generate AI sentences, and produce the progress reports and evaluation summaries clinicians use. Nothing else.

## What we never do
- ❌ Sell, rent, or trade student data
- ❌ Targeted or behavioral advertising to students
- ❌ Build commercial profiles of children
- ❌ Re-disclose data except to the service providers below, or as required by law

## FERPA
When a school uses Speak, we act as a **"school official"** with a **"legitimate educational interest"** under FERPA — performing a service under the school's direct control, using education records only as the school authorizes, and never re-disclosing them.

## COPPA (under-13)
For students under 13, we rely on the **school's consent** on behalf of parents (consistent with FTC guidance for educational technology), and we use that data **solely for the educational purpose** the school authorizes. Parents may, through their school, review or request deletion of their child's data.

## Security
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
- Row-level security and role-based, least-privilege access
- Data minimization — we only collect what the service needs
- Account and data deletion available on request

## Service providers (subprocessors)
We use **Supabase** (database), **Anthropic / Claude** (AI sentence generation), **Netlify** (hosting), and **Stripe** (payments — no student data). All are bound to protect data consistent with our commitments. The AI feature sends the *words a user selects* to Anthropic to build a sentence; we do not send student names to it.

## Your rights & control
Schools and families own their student data. You may request access, correction, export, or deletion at any time. We sign Data Privacy Agreements (including the SDPC National DPA) with districts.
