**1.7 Term.** The Company shall continue in existence until dissolved pursuant to this Agreement or applicable law.

---

## ARTICLE II — MEMBERSHIP

**2.1 Sole Member.** Logan J. Snell is the sole Member of the Company, holding 100% of the membership interest.

**2.2 Additional Members.** No additional member may be admitted to the Company without the prior written consent of the Member. Admission of any additional member shall require an amendment to this Agreement signed by all parties.

**2.3 Membership Interest.** The membership interest is personal property. The Member may transfer or assign his membership interest only in accordance with this Agreement and applicable Kansas law.

**2.4 Liability.** The Member shall not be personally liable for any debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a Member, except as required by the Kansas LLC Act.

---

## ARTICLE III — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

**3.1 Initial Contribution.** The Member has made or shall make an initial capital contribution to the Company in an amount to be determined and recorded on the Company's books.

**3.2 Additional Contributions.** The Member may, but is not required to, make additional capital contributions to the Company at any time.

**3.3 No Interest.** No interest shall accrue on any capital contribution unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

## ARTICLE IV — MANAGEMENT

**4.1 Member-Managed.** The Company shall be managed by its Member. The Member has full, exclusive, and complete authority, power, and discretion to manage and control the business, affairs, and properties of the Company.

**4.2 Authority.** Without limiting the generality of Section 4.1, the Member is authorized, on behalf of the Company, to:

(a) open and maintain bank accounts, credit accounts, and financial instruments;  
(b) enter into contracts, agreements, licenses, and joint ventures;  
(c) hire and terminate employees, independent contractors, and advisors;  
(d) file, prosecute, maintain, license, assign, and abandon intellectual property applications and registrations, including patent applications (provisional and non-provisional), trademark applications, and copyright registrations;  
(e) apply for and accept grants, loans, and other funding;  
(f) purchase, lease, sell, or encumber assets;  
(g) incur debt and execute instruments on behalf of the Company; and  
(h) take any other action the Member deems necessary or appropriate for the conduct of the Company's business.

**4.3 Officers.** The Member may appoint officers of the Company (including, without limitation, a Chief Executive Officer, Chief Technology Officer, or Secretary) and may remove such officers at any time. As of the effective date of this Agreement, Logan J. Snell serves as Chief Executive Officer.

---

## ARTICLE V — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**5.1 Ownership by the Company.** All intellectual property created, developed, conceived, or reduced to practice by the Member, any employee, or any contractor in connection with the business of the Company, including but not limited to:

- software code (source and object code),
- inventions, discoveries, and improvements (whether or not patentable),
- patent applications (provisional and non-provisional), patents, and patent rights,
- trade secrets, proprietary methods, and know-how,
- trademarks, service marks, trade names, and logos,
- copyrightable works, and
- all other intellectual property,

shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

**5.2 Assignment by Member.** To the extent any intellectual property described in Section 5.1 is vested in the Member personally rather than the Company (including any patent rights arising from inventions conceived by the Member), the Member hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys all right, title, and interest in such intellectual property to the Company. This includes any and all provisional patent applications filed in the Member's name in connection with the Company's business.

**5.3 IP Agreements with Third Parties.** Prior to commencing any work for the Company, every employee, contractor, advisor, or other person who may contribute to or have access to the Company's intellectual property shall execute a written agreement assigning all resulting intellectual property to the Company and containing appropriate confidentiality provisions.

**5.4 Company Name & Brand.** The trade name "Speak AAC," the trademark "Speak AAC™," the domain speakaac.org, and all associated brand assets are the property of the Company.

---

## ARTICLE VI — DISTRIBUTIONS & TAXATION

**6.1 Distributions.** The Member may withdraw funds from the Company or cause the Company to make distributions at such times and in such amounts as the Member determines in his sole discretion, subject to applicable law and the Company's obligations.

**6.2 Tax Treatment.** The Company is a single-member LLC and shall be treated as a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes (unless the Member elects otherwise). The Company's income, losses, deductions, and credits shall be reported on the Member's individual federal income tax return (Schedule C of Form 1040) or such other form as required by applicable law.

**6.3 Tax Records.** The Company shall maintain adequate records to support all tax filings, including records of income, expenses, asset purchases, and payroll.

**6.4 Estimated Taxes.** The Member shall be responsible for making estimated tax payments on his share of Company income as required by federal and Kansas law.

---

## ARTICLE VII — BOOKS, RECORDS & BANKING

**7.1 Books and Records.** The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books of account and records of its business and affairs, which shall be available for inspection by the Member at any time.

**7.2 Banking.** The Company shall maintain one or more bank accounts in its name, separate from the personal accounts of the Member. The Member shall not commingle personal and Company funds.

**7.3 Fiscal Year.** The Company's fiscal year shall be the calendar year (January 1 – December 31) unless otherwise elected.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — DISSOLUTION & WINDING UP

**8.1 Dissolution Events.** The Company shall be dissolved upon:

(a) the written determination of the Member to dissolve;  
(b) the entry of a judicial decree of dissolution; or  
(c) any other event requiring dissolution under the Kansas LLC Act.

**8.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Member (or a designated liquidator) shall wind up the Company's affairs: pay or provide for all debts and liabilities, then distribute remaining assets to the Member.

---

## ARTICLE IX — MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Kansas, including the Kansas LLC Act (K.S.A. 17-76,134 et seq.).

**9.2 Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Member.

**9.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**9.4 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement of the Member with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations.

**9.5 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and electronic signatures shall be binding.
