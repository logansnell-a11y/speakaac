The clinical difference

A safety channel, built in.

Speak lets a client discreetly signal pain, fear, or distress directly to a trusted contact — a capability standard AAC devices simply don't have. Children with communication disabilities are abused at 3–4× the rate of their peers; the thing that makes them vulnerable is the same thing that keeps them silent. Speak addresses that from day one, and it's in the free tier — always.
