User Profile

Run the setup wizard again to reconfigure for a new user or update the current profile.

Adult / Elder Profile

For adults with ALS, stroke aphasia, Parkinson's, MS, or any condition affecting speech. Safety alerts use adult-appropriate language and large targets are automatically enabled.

Safety alerts will say "A resident is asking for help" rather than child-specific defaults. Incident reports use resident-appropriate language for clinical documentation.

Simple 4-Button Layout

Shows only 4 large symbols at a time — best for young children, tremor, or cognitive impairment. Use the ◀ ▶ arrows to page through symbols. Automatically enables large targets.

Eye Gaze Control

When running on the platform device, Speak can be fully controlled by eye gaze — no touch required. Requires the gaze tracking service to be running on the device (port 5050). Look at a symbol and hold your gaze to select it.

How long to hold your gaze before a symbol is selected. Higher = less likely to accidentally select while reading. Default 1.8s.

Calibrate once per user or whenever their seating position changes. Opens a full-screen dot sequence — takes about 15 seconds.

Switch Scanning

For users who can't reliably touch the screen. Speak highlights each symbol in turn — press the switch (or Space / Enter) to select the highlighted one. Works with any switch that sends a keypress.

How long each symbol stays highlighted before the scan moves on. Slower = easier to time the switch.

Auditory scanning — for users with low vision, Speak says each option aloud as it's highlighted.

Fixed Layout (Motor Planning)

Keeps every symbol in the same place, every time — no pages, no "More" button to expand. Consistent positions help the user build motor memory and find words faster, the way you learn to touch-type without looking. Recommended for users with apraxia or anyone learning a stable layout.

Trusted Safety Contact

This person receives a private alert when the user presses the Help button — bypassing the primary caretaker. Use a grandparent, teacher, therapist, or other trusted adult.

⚠ The private safety channel is not a substitute for emergency services. In an immediate emergency, always call 911 first.

Therapist / Teacher Access

Clinic and Institution plans: give your SLP, therapist, or teacher read-only access to this child's communication log in the Therapist Dashboard.

Cloud Sync

Sign in to save your settings to the cloud — so they're never lost if you switch devices or clear your browser.

Delete your account?

My Symbols

Upload photos of people, pets, or places that matter to this child. Each photo becomes a tappable symbol in the "Mine" category — tap it to speak its name aloud.

Language

Useful for bilingual classrooms, SLPs working with Spanish-speaking children, and families bridging two languages. Speech output follows the language selected above.

Voice

Kiosk Mode

Locks the app in fullscreen. The child cannot exit or access other apps. Exit requires entering your Provider PIN. Recommended for school and clinic tablets.

Speak
A voice for every child

Speak is a free AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) app built for nonverbal and minimally verbal children and adults. It gives them symbols, audio, and AI-assisted sentences to communicate — and a private safety channel to report abuse to a trusted adult without anyone in the room knowing.
