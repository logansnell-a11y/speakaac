Engineering Design Process — Speak AAC · speakaac.org

Logan Snell · Axtell High School · 2026

1
Step 1 — Ask
Identify the Need
"What problem actually needs solving?"

The Problem

  • AAC devices cost $6,000–$8,000 — most families are denied by insurance
  • Existing apps require a specific device (iPad only) and an expert to set up
  • No free, browser-based option existed
  • No AAC tool had a private safety channel for children to report abuse

Constraints

  • Must work on any device — phone, tablet, Chromebook, laptop
  • No app store required
  • Core features must be free, forever
  • Must be safe for children — HIPAA-aware, privacy-first
  • One developer, no team, no funding to start
2
Step 2 — Research
Investigate the Problem
"What already exists? What's missing?"

Existing Solutions

  • Proloquo2Go — $300+, iOS only, no safety channel
  • TouchChat HD — $150+, per-device purchase, no AI
  • LAMP Words for Life — $300, iOS only
  • All require specialists, months of manual setup

Key Research Findings

  • Mulberry Symbols — free, open-source AAC symbols (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  • WebSpeech API — free text-to-speech built into every browser
  • Supabase — free database for user accounts and sync
  • 17 diagnosis profiles needed: Autism, Down syndrome, Cerebral Palsy, and more
  • HIPAA BAA required for clinical use
3
Step 3 — Imagine
Develop Possible Solutions
"What could this look like?"

Options Considered

  • Native iOS/Android app vs browser-based PWA
  • Paid upfront vs subscription vs free core + paid clinic tier
  • Manual symbol entry vs pre-built diagnosis profiles
  • Static symbols vs AI-assisted sentence building
  • No safety feature vs private silent alert channel

The Winning Idea

  • Browser-based — works everywhere, no install
  • Setup in under 2 minutes via diagnosis profile
  • AI (Claude by Anthropic) builds sentences from symbols
  • Hidden safety button sends silent alert to trusted adult
  • Free forever for families, affordable for clinics
4
Step 4 — Plan
Select a Promising Solution
"Build the blueprint before building the product."

Technical Stack

  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS — no frameworks, fast on any device
  • Symbols: Mulberry CC BY-SA 4.0 — 3,000+ free symbols
  • Auth + sync: Supabase — free tier, row-level security
  • Hosting: Netlify — free CDN, auto-deploy from GitHub
  • AI: Claude API via secure Netlify function
  • Payments: Stripe — $49.99/mo clinic, $499/yr annual

Business Plan

  • Free for families — always
  • Clinic plan: 30-day free trial → $499/yr
  • HIPAA BAA available for healthcare providers
  • Grant path: NIDILRR, SBIR, Kansas Health Foundation
  • LLC formation for legal protection
5
Step 5 — Create
Build the Prototype
"Turn the plan into something real."

What Was Built

  • Symbol grid — color-coded categories, tap to speak
  • 17 diagnosis profiles — auto-configures layout and vocabulary
  • AI sentence builder — turns symbol taps into full sentences
  • Private safety channel — silent alert to trusted contact
  • Kiosk mode — locks app for tablet use in schools
  • Provider dashboard — SLPs manage multiple clients

By the Numbers

  • Started: February 26, 2026
  • Live at: speakaac.org
  • Pages built: 18 HTML pages
  • Stripe payments: active and processing
  • Analytics: GA4 tracking real usage
  • Total cost to launch: ~$0
6
Step 6 — Test
Evaluate the Prototype
"Does it actually solve the problem?"

What Was Tested

  • Real users on phones, tablets, Chromebooks — all worked
  • Onboarding flow — timed at under 2 minutes per profile
  • Safety channel — alert delivered via email successfully
  • AI sentences — tested across 17 diagnosis profiles
  • Stripe payments — end-to-end purchase and account upgrade verified

Feedback Received

  • Pricing was too complex — 5 tiers → simplified to 3
  • Symbol licensing concern — migrated from ARASAAC to Mulberry
  • Nav was inconsistent across pages — standardized all 18 pages
  • Default PIN was a security risk — forced PIN creation on setup
  • Color scheme unclear — switched to consistent blue brand
7
Step 7 — Improve
Redesign as Needed
"Engineering never really stops."

Already Improved

  • Symbol system migrated — legally clean, globally accessible
  • Pricing restructured — free trial removes every sales objection
  • Security hardened — PIN required, RLS policies, 2FA on all accounts
  • Analytics added — tracking symbol usage, AI, safety channel in real time
  • Terms of service updated — prohibited uses, 14 sections

Still Improving

  • LLC filing → legal business entity
  • Cold outreach to SLPs and clinics
  • Grant applications — NIDILRR, KHF $5k, SBIR Phase I
  • SAM.gov registration for federal contracts
  • The cycle continues — ship, learn, improve, repeat
