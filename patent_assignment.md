## HOW TO FILE THIS WITH USPTO

After your PPA is granted an Application Number:

1. Go to **assignments.uspto.gov**
2. Click "Submit Assignment for Recording"
3. Upload this signed Assignment as a PDF
4. Enter the Application Number
5. Pay the recording fee (currently $0 online / $50 paper)
6. Save the confirmation number and recorded copy

Recording the assignment creates a public record that Speak AAC LLC owns the patent rights, which protects you from any future dispute and is required for licensing or selling the patent.

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

The PPA must list Logan J. Snell as inventor (because he conceived the invention). But as a sole-member LLC owner, you want the *business entity* to own the patent rights — not you personally — for:

- **Liability protection:** Patent enforcement lawsuits go through the LLC, not you personally
- **Investor clarity:** Investors and grant agencies expect IP to be owned by the company
- **Business sale:** If you ever sell Speak, the patent transfers with the company
- **Tax efficiency:** Royalty income flows through the LLC

This assignment accomplishes all of that while keeping your inventorship record accurate.
